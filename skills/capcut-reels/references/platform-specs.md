# Platform specs

Specs change; treat these as sensible defaults and follow the user's instructions
if they differ.

## Formats
- TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps
  (match the source footage fps if it's 25 or 60).
- YouTube standard: horizontal 16:9, 1920x1080 or higher.

## Length guidance
- Mood or text clips: often 6 to 15 seconds; built to loop.
- Football edits and highlights: about 15 to 45 seconds.
- Painting process: about 20 to 60 seconds for shorts; longer for YouTube tutorials.
- Startup explainers: about 30 to 60 seconds for shorts; longer on YouTube.

## Safe zone for vertical video (1080x1920)
Keep text and key action inside the central area. Approximate areas to avoid:
- Top: roughly the first 250 px (app header and search).
- Bottom: roughly the last 400 to 480 px (caption, username, music label).
- Right: roughly the last 120 to 150 px (like, comment, share buttons).
When unsure, keep important text in the middle third vertically.
