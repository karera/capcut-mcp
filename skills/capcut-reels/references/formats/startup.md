# Format: startup and project videos

Talking about a startup or project, explaining what it is, why it matters, and
building in public.

## What makes it work
A clear problem, a credible person, and one idea the viewer remembers. Clarity
beats production value, but tight editing makes the founder look sharp.

## Structure (short explainer)
1. Hook: the problem in one line, a surprising fact, or a bold claim. No "hi, I'm...".
2. Problem: who has it and why it hurts, in a sentence or two.
3. Solution: what the product does, shown not told (screen recording, demo, product shot).
4. Proof: a result, a user reaction, a number, or progress so far.
5. Call to action: try it, follow the journey, or join the waitlist.

Build-in-public variant: what I did this week, what happened, what I learned.

## Editing choices
- Talking head: tighten hard. Remove pauses and filler, and use punch-ins to hide
  the cuts (see `editing-craft.md`).
- Cover a lot with b-roll: screen recordings, product shots, workspace, the team.
  When the founder mentions something, show it.
- Captions on throughout, with key words highlighted; many viewers watch muted.
- Text callouts for key numbers or claims.
- Clean, modern look: minimal transitions, subtle music well under the voice.
- Screen recordings: zoom into the part that matters, and keep them short.

## Honesty
Don't add claims, numbers, or testimonials the user didn't provide. If a line in
the script overstates something, flag it rather than amplifying it.
