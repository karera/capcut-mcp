# Format: football edits

Raw footage plus short, fun inserts to make it entertaining.

## Rights first
Footage the user filmed themselves (matches, training, skills, fans) is safe to
use. Clips from TV broadcasts or league footage are usually copyrighted and often
get muted or taken down. If the source is unclear, flag it in the finishing notes.

## What makes it work
Energy, timing, and payoff. The best moment should feel earned and land on the beat.

## Structure
1. Hook: open on the goal, skill, or reaction itself, or a teaser of it, then
   rewind to the build-up.
2. Build-up: quick cuts of the play developing, cut on the beat.
3. Payoff: the key moment, played at full length with room to land.
4. Reaction: celebration, crowd, or a funny insert.
5. Replay or end: an alternate angle or slow-motion replay, or a quick loop.

## Fun inserts
Short clips that add humour or emphasis: reactions, sound bites, a freeze frame,
a zoom on a face. Keep each insert brief and place it right after the moment
it's reacting to. Use them sparingly; the football is the star.

## Editing choices
- Cut on music beats; save the biggest moment for the drop.
- Speed ramps (slow-motion on the key touch) and zoom-ins on the key moment are the
  signature techniques. Speed is available through `capcut_set_props`, but verify
  timing in CapCut. Smooth speed ramps usually need CapCut's curve tools; note them.
- Punch-in on the key moment (125% or more) for emphasis.
- Text: minimal and punchy (a name, a score, one-word reactions), in the safe zone.
- Sound effects: one impact on the key moment is enough.
